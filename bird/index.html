FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Can't find what you need? Our team replies within a few hours.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are fully covered in under ten minutes. Sign in with your workspace account and Bluebird discovers everything else on its own.

Will it slow down our devices?

No. The device agent uses less memory than a browser tab and does its heavy thinking in our EU cloud.

Where is our data stored?

Only in EU data centres in Frankfurt and Prague, encrypted with keys you can rotate or revoke at any time.

Can we try it before paying?

Yes. Fledgling is free forever for up to five users, and Flock comes with a 14-day trial, no card required.

What happens if the AI gets it wrong?

Every automatic action can be undone in one click, and you can require human approval for anything you choose.
